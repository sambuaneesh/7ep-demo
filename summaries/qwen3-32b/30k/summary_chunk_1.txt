## **5. Key Business Logic and Algorithms**
### **AutoInsuranceProcessor Logic**
Determines insurance premiums, warning letters, and policy status based on:
- **Claims Count (0 to ≥5)**.
- **Driver Age (16–85)**.

**Rules**:
1. **0 Claims**:
   - Age 16–25: $50 premium, no warning.
   - Age 26–85: $25 premium, no warning.
2. **1 Claim**:
   - Age 16–25: $100 premium, LTR1.
   - Age 26–85: $50 premium, no warning.
3. **2–4 Claims**:
   - Age 16–25: $400 premium, LTR2.
   - Age 26–85: $200 premium, LTR3.
4. **≥5 Claims**: Policy canceled.
5. **Invalid Input**:
   - Age <16 or >85/Claims <0: Error response (`isError = true`).

**Implementation Details**:
- Uses nested `if-else` conditions for rule evaluation.
- Returns `AutoInsuranceAction` objects encapsulating results.
